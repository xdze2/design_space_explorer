type: function

**Why it matters.** A beam that is not perpendicular to the Y axis turns circles into ovals, and a beam that twists when driven from one side makes lines wobble. A long Y span driven from a single side wants to skew.

**Tradeoffs.** Mechanical fixes are cheap but add a part to align; electronic fixes need a second motor and driver and tuning so that both sides stay in step.
