**Why choose it.** A hobby servo swings an arm to raise and lower the pen. Cheap, light, one signal wire. It only sets the up and down positions; how hard the pen presses is a sub-function of its own. The AxiDraw and the EasyDraw both work this way.

**Tradeoffs.** It needs firmware that can drive it as a tool, and its speed limits how fast a stroke can start. It is the wear part of the machine: extra pen weight, a spring or a sticky slide wears it out early (the EasyDraw ships a spare and expects one a year for heavy use). Coreless or brushless servos lift faster and vibrate less.
