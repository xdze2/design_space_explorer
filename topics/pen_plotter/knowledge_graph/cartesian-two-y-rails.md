**Why choose it.** The easiest layout to build and tune: one motor rides on the moving X carriage, one fixed motor drives Y, with a rail on each side of the bed. It is the layout of most hobby reference builds and is well documented.

**Tradeoffs.** Moving mass is higher because a motor rides on the gantry. The two rails must be parallel and equally preloaded over their whole length, and a Y axis driven from one side wants to rack. Besides the drive, the transmission, the guides (the two rails are the guides of the Y axis) and a way to hold the surface, it therefore brings a sub-function of its own: keeping the gantry square. It costs less than CoreXY, but the motor riding on the gantry makes it accelerate more slowly.
