**Why choose it.** The pen sits in a holder pushed down by a spring. The lift lets it go, the spring sets the force on the paper and a hard stop sets the travel. Works in any orientation, and the compliance absorbs paper that is not flat. AxiDraw's brushless upgrade adds a spring for pens that need pressure.

**Tradeoffs.** One force per spring: another pen may need another spring. The lift must fight the spring to raise the pen, which loads a small servo.
