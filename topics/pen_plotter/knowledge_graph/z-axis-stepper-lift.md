**Why choose it.** A third stepper on a short screw or belt moves the pen holder up and down by position, as on the iDraw. Any lift height, a controlled push down, enough travel for thick media and for picking pens from a rack, and depth control for brushes.

**Tradeoffs.** An extra motor, driver and axis to home, a heavier carriage, and a slower lift than a solenoid. The controller needs a third stepper channel.
