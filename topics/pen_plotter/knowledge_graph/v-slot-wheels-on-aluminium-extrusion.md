**Why choose it.** The cheapest guide and the easiest to source: wheels ride in the groove of the extrusion that is also the frame, with eccentric nuts to adjust the preload.

**Tradeoffs.** It needs careful adjustment to remove play without binding, and it is less stiff than rails. Wheels wear and flat-spot over time, but for a pen load they last. Residual play and flex show up as wobble in the lines. The EasyDraw V4 uses them on an aluminium extrusion frame with a metal gantry, supported on all four sides (see Delrin V-wheels).
