summary: "Steel rails with bearing blocks: low play, low friction and stiff, but dearer and heavier"
---
**Why choose it.** Low play and low friction out of the box, and much stiffer, which raises the resonance and allows more acceleration. Two spaced bearing blocks also make a good wide carriage for a cantilever.

**Tradeoffs.** More expensive and heavier, and the rails need a flat, accurate mounting surface; mounted on bent extrusion they bind. Most of the gain shows only if the rest of the machine is equally rigid. The low play also gives straighter lines and better repeatability.
