**The limit.** Which tools the machine must hold and use: fineliners, felt markers, ballpoints and gel pens, pencils, brushes, and their diameter and weight.

**How solutions fare.** Felt tips want a light, constant force, and the pen's weight is enough. Ballpoints and pencils need more force, from a spring or a Z push. Brushes need depth control or a controlled force (a Z stepper or a voice coil), since their stroke changes with pressure, and they must be reloaded, which favours a rack or a manual pause. Heavy or thick tools need a bigger holder and a stronger lift. The list of tools belongs to a design document.
