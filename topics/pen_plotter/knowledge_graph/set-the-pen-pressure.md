**Why it matters.** The force at the tip sets the line: too little and ballpoints skip, too much and felt tips spread or the paper tears. With a brush, the stroke width is its depth. The force should stay the same across the sheet, whatever its flatness.

**Tradeoffs.** A passive force (a spring or the pen's weight) is cheap and absorbs uneven media but cannot change during a drawing. A set depth gives control but passes every bump of the surface to the tip. Shared by the servo, solenoid and Z-stepper lifts.
