**Why it matters.** The surface must not move under the pen, must be flat so the pressure is even across the sheet, and is where the media is loaded and aligned.

**Tradeoffs.** A fixed bed is the flattest and simplest but limits the media to what fits on it. An open frame takes any support but has to be aligned to it each time. A wall mount saves floor space but rules out gravity pen pressure. Shared by the gantry layouts and the arm; the polargraph and the grit wheel hold the media themselves.
