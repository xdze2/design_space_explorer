**The limit.** How good the drawn line must look: straight, free of wobble, with circles that stay round and strokes that start and stop cleanly. It is limited by play, rigidity, belt stretch and the pen interface, far more than by the theoretical step resolution.

**How solutions fare.** Choices that remove play and add stiffness (rails, a square beam, both ends driven) help; choices that add slack or flex (long overhangs, loose wheels, screw wobble) hurt. A repeatable pen lift matters as much as any axis choice. How good is good enough belongs to a design document, not to the graph.
