**Why choose it.** The pen and its holder ride on a low-friction vertical slide. The lift only raises it, and the weight of the pen presses on the paper, as on the AxiDraw. The simplest and gentlest option, with a constant force.

**Tradeoffs.** Light pens stay up against the slide friction. Pens that need force (ballpoints, pencils) need added weight, which wears the servo, and the drop can bounce at high speed. It only works on a horizontal surface, so the wall-mounted frame and the polargraph exclude it (declared on them). It costs nothing extra: there is no spring or added part.
