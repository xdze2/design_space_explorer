Delrin (acetal) wheels with a V profile, riding in the groove of the aluminium extrusion. The EasyDraw V4 moves on Delrin V-wheels with NEMA 17 steppers.

**Why choose it.** Cheap, quiet and self-lubricating, with no rail to buy: the extrusion is the track.

**Tradeoffs.** Repeatability depends on play and wear: the wheels need their preload set once, and the plastic slowly flat-spots, which is fine for a pen load.
