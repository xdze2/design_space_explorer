**The limit.** The amount a builder is willing to spend on parts. It acts on almost every choice, but mostly on the structure, the motors and the power supply, and on how much is spent on drivers and guides.

**How solutions fare.** A polargraph, a servo arm or geared steppers are the cheapest end; plain Cartesian or an H-bot with wheels on extrusion, a servo and a hobby board come next. CoreXY adds belts and idlers, linear rails and a wireless board add more, a second Y motor or a Z stepper adds a driver, and an automatic pen changer adds a mechanism. Shipping on long extrusion can matter as much as the parts. The actual budget belongs to a design document, not to the graph.
