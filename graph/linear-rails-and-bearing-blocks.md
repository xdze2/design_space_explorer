**Why choose it.** Low play and low friction out of the box, and much stiffer, which raises the resonance and allows more acceleration. Two spaced bearing blocks also make a good wide carriage for a cantilever.

**Tradeoffs.** More expensive and heavier, and the rails need a flat, accurate mounting surface; mounted on bent extrusion they bind. Most of the gain shows only if the rest of the machine is equally rigid.
