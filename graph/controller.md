**Why it matters.** The controller runs the firmware that turns G-code into steps. It decides the workflow (how drawings are sent), the available acceleration tuning and kinematics support.

**Tradeoffs.** Mainstream hobby boards are cheap and well documented. Boards with Wi-Fi add convenience but need more configuration.
