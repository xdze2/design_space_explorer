**Why it matters.** Pen pressure, tip compliance and a repeatable up/down travel affect line quality more than a 0.05 mm mechanical improvement.

**Tradeoffs.** Simple lifters are cheap but give little control over pressure. A spring-loaded holder with a repeatable stop helps with any of them.
