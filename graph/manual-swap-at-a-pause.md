**Why choose it.** The plot pauses, someone swaps the pen and resumes. Every controller can do it, and multi-colour EasyDraw drawings are made this way. No extra parts.

**Tradeoffs.** Someone must be there for each colour. Registration depends on the holder seating every pen at the same place and height; removing a pen can nudge the carriage.
