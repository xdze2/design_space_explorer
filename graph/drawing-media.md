**The limit.** What the drawing goes on: thin paper, heavy watercolour paper, card, a notebook, wood or another rigid panel, and its thickness and flatness.

**How solutions fare.** A fixed bed takes sheets up to its clear height; an open frame takes any support it can stand over; a grit wheel takes only thin, flexible sheets. Thick or warped media need pen travel and compliance (a spring or the pen's weight) rather than a rigid depth. Absorbent paper bleeds, so the media also sets line quality. The media itself belongs to a design document.
