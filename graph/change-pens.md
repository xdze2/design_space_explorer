**Why it matters.** Colour drawings and different line widths need several tools, and a brush needs reloading. Every change risks putting the next pen off the lines of the previous one.

**Tradeoffs.** Manual changes cost nothing but someone's time. Automatic changers add mechanism and calibration, and each pen gets its own offset.
