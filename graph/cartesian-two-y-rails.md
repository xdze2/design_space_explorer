**Why choose it.** The easiest layout to build and tune: one motor rides on the moving X carriage, one fixed motor drives Y, with a rail on each side of the bed. It is the layout of most hobby reference builds and is well documented.

**Tradeoffs.** Moving mass is higher because a motor rides on the gantry. The two rails must be parallel and equally preloaded over their whole length, and a Y axis driven from one side wants to rack. It therefore brings a sub-function of its own, keeping the gantry square, besides the way motion is transmitted and the way the axes are guided (the two rails are the guides of the Y axis).
