**Why choose it.** The easiest layout to build and tune: one motor on the moving X carriage, one fixed motor on Y, with a rail on each side of the bed. This is the base of the reference BOM (2020 V-slot, about $150).

**Tradeoffs.** Moving mass is higher because a motor rides on the gantry. The two rails must be parallel and equally preloaded over 500 mm, and a Y axis driven from one side wants to rack. Fix it with a stiff rod linking both belts, or a second synchronized motor.
