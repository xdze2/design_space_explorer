**Why choose it.** Cheap (about $15 for belt, pulleys and idlers), light and fast. 20T pulleys with 1/16 microstepping give 80 steps/mm.

**Tradeoffs.** Belt stretch and tooth engagement limit accuracy to 0.1 to 0.3 mm. Use reinforced-core belt, wrap enough teeth round each pulley and keep belts tight and straight.
