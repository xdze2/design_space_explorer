**Why choose it.** The gantry hangs on a wall or an easel, like the iDraw H A1: large formats without the floor space, and the drawing can be watched as it grows.

**Tradeoffs.** Gravity pulls the carriage along the vertical axis, so that motor holds the weight even at rest and loses position if power drops.

**Links.** It excludes the pen weight on a free slide: on a vertical surface the pen no longer falls onto it.
