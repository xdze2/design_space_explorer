A cheap, home-built pen plotter with an A3 drawing area (420 x 297 mm), around $150 of parts. Five decisions shape the build: the gantry geometry, the linear guide, the drive type, the pen lift and the controller. Accuracy in practice comes more from rigidity, play removal and the pen interface than from any single component choice.
