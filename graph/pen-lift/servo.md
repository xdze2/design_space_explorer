**Why choose it.** About $4, with a repeatable up/down travel set by the servo angle. Pair it with a spring-loaded holder for compliance. GRBL needs a servo-capable fork, and FluidNC supports it natively.

**Tradeoffs.** Little control over contact pressure, and some play in the horn. Only two positions, so no pressure variation.
