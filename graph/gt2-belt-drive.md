**Why choose it.** Cheap, light and fast. With common pulleys and fine microstepping it gives plenty of resolution for drawing, and it suits every belt-based layout.

**Tradeoffs.** Belt stretch and tooth engagement limit real accuracy well below the theoretical resolution. Use a reinforced-core belt, wrap enough teeth around each pulley and keep belts tight and straight.
