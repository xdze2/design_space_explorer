**Why choose it.** A hobby servo swings an arm to raise and lower the pen. A spring-loaded holder with a repeatable stop sets the pen pressure, and the servo only sets up and down travel. Cheap, light and takes one signal wire.

**Tradeoffs.** The servo needs firmware that can drive it as a tool, and its speed limits how fast a stroke can start. Pen pressure is only as repeatable as the stop and the spring.
