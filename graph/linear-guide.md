**Why it matters.** The guide defines how much play and friction each axis has. Removing play while keeping smooth motion is the main skill of the build: too tight binds and loses steps, too loose gives wobbly lines.

**Tradeoffs.** Cheap guides need adjustment and are less stiff. Precision rails cost more and need flatter mounting surfaces.
