**Why choose it.** The cheapest option (about $12 for wheels, brackets and plates, with the extrusion doubling as the rail) and easy to source. Eccentric nuts allow adjusting out wheel play.

**Tradeoffs.** Play and friction depend on careful adjustment, and the stiffness is limited. A 500 mm 2020 beam is about the practical limit for A3.
