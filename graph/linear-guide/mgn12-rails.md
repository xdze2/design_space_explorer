**Why choose it.** Precise, low play and smooth, with high moment capacity from the carriage block. A good fit for a single-rail Y, where the carriage takes a tilting load.

**Tradeoffs.** Costs more and needs a flat, stiff mounting surface. Together with TMC2209 drivers it moves the total from about $150 to about $250.
