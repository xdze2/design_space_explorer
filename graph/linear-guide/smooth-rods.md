**Why choose it.** Cheap, common (3D printer heritage) and easy to find, and fine for light moving parts.

**Tradeoffs.** Rods sag over long spans and have less stiffness than a rail. Bearings can bind if the rods are not parallel.

**Constraints.** It excludes the single-rail cantilever (`gantry/single-rail-cantilever`): a round rod resists the tilting moment of a cantilevered beam poorly and the carriage would rock.
