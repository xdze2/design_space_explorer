**The limit.** The amount a builder is willing to spend on parts. It acts on almost every choice, but mostly on the structure, the motors and the power supply, and on how much is spent on drivers and guides.

**How solutions fare.** Plain Cartesian with wheels on extrusion, a servo, and a hobby board is the cheap end. CoreXY adds belts and idlers, linear rails and a wireless board add more, and a second Y motor adds a driver. Shipping on long extrusion can matter as much as the parts. The actual budget belongs to a design document, not to the graph.
