**Why choose it.** The office plotter layout (HP 7470): grit-covered rollers with pinch wheels move the paper along one axis, and the pen carriage moves along a rail on the other. Compact, the machine is no wider than the paper, and the sheet length is nearly unlimited (rolls).

**Tradeoffs.** Only thin, flexible media that can be pinched and rolled: no thick card, wood or bound notebook. Registration depends on grip, so any slip shows in the drawing. The rollers hold the sheet, so it brings the drive and the guide of the carriage, but no other way to hold the surface.
