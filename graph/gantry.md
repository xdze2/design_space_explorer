**Why it matters.** The geometry sets the moving mass, the risk of racking (the beam skewing when driven from one side) and how open the machine is for loading paper. Acceleration, and so drawing speed, is limited by moving mass and resonance, not by motor torque.

**Tradeoffs.** Simple layouts are easy to tune but carry a motor on the gantry. Lighter layouts need more belts, idlers and careful tension.
