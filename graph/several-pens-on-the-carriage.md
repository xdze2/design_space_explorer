**Why choose it.** Two or more pens side by side on the head, each with its own lift, and the firmware applies an offset per pen. No pick and place and fast changes.

**Tradeoffs.** More moving mass (one lift per pen), few colours, a drawing area reduced by the offsets, and an offset per pen to calibrate. Idle pens are uncapped and dry.
