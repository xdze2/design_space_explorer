A small hobby servo (the common blue SG90 or the metal-gear MG90S). Light enough for the carriage; metal gears survive repeated pen changes better. It is the one wear part of most servo-lift plotters: the EasyDraw ships a spare MG90S, the AxiDraw uses an SG90.
