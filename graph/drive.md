**Why it matters.** The drive converts motor rotation into travel and sets resolution, speed and belt stretch or backlash. Resolution is not accuracy: 80 steps/mm is 0.0125 mm in theory, but real accuracy is around 0.1 to 0.3 mm.

**Tradeoffs.** Belts are fast, cheap and light but stretch. Screws are precise and slow.
