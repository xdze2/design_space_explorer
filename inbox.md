

## Pen plotters to look for

1. AxiDraw V3 / SE/A3 (Evil Mad Scientist), the reference commercial plotter
2. AxiDraw MiniKit (DIY kit version)
3. iDraw 2.0 (UUNA TEK), AxiDraw-like clone
4. EleksDraw (EleksMaker), cheap laser/pen CNC-style plotter
5. EasyDraw V4 (MakerQ), already in the inbox
6. Bachin T-A3 / Bachin Draw, cheap A3 plotter
7. WaterColorBot / Eggbot family (Evil Mad Scientist)
8. Makelangelo (Marginally Clever), hanging polargraph
9. Polargraph / Hektor (Sandy Noble), wall-hanging
10. Scribit (Bitcraze/Scribit), wall-mounted whiteboard robot
11. HP 7475A / HP 7550 (vintage flatbed/sheet-fed plotters)
12. Roland DXY-1300 / DXY-990 (vintage desktop plotters)
13. Prusa MK3 / Ender 3 with pen holder mod (3D printer as plotter)
14. Pen plotter from a CD/DVD drive, two steppers (classic DIY)
15. Open-source CoreXY plotter builds (e.g. "Penplotter" by Maker's Muse / Thingiverse)
